## Summary

Modernizes OPAL with a dashboard, command center, themes, and local productivity tools.

## Description

OPAL Redesign is a browser extension for Firefox and Chrome/Chromium that makes the OPAL learning platform cleaner, faster, and easier to use.

Features:

- Modern dashboard with widgets for courses, favorites, calendar, deadlines, canteen menus, news, and more
- Command center with local search for courses, pages, folders, and files
- Local search index for quickly finding OPAL content again
- File and course-section detection on OPAL pages
- Dark, light, and OLED themes with accent color customization
- Options page for exporting, importing, and deleting local extension data

Privacy:

The extension processes OPAL data primarily locally in your browser. It does not operate its own backend server for OPAL data. On first run, setup asks you to accept the terms and acknowledge the privacy policy; every extra feature, including background preloading, deadline detection, and the canteen menu, stays off until you turn it on. Local data such as settings, dashboard layout, and search index metadata can be deleted from the options page.

Important language requirement:

OPAL must be set to German. The extension relies on German OPAL interface text and labels to find menus, buttons, portlets, and course content reliably. Other OPAL interface languages are not officially supported and may break parts of the extension.

Disclaimer:

OPAL Redesign is not an official product of OPAL, BPS Bildungsportal Sachsen, TU Dresden, or any other university. Deadlines and exam information detected by the extension are unofficial and may be incomplete or wrong; only OPAL and your university are binding.
